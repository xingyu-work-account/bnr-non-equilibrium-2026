## Gap inventory

The native interval is the centred rolling median of 101 intervals, because the NH4 logging rate changes from 20 s to 2 s. Intervals longer than 2.0x the native interval are classed `gap`; intervals longer than 1.5x are listed as `missing_sample`. A relative tolerance of 0.0001 absorbs timestamp rounding. Contiguous flagged intervals are merged into one row. `partial_stamp_artifact` marks runs inside the first or last NH4/ORP stamp: a partial stamp (for example 22 rows at 20 s, i.e. 0.005 d of data) is stretched over the full 0.01 d by the prescribed reconstruction, so its intervals look about twice the native interval. These are consequences of the stated assumption, not missing data.

## Stated assumptions

1. NH4 and ORP timestamps are rounded to 0.01 d; rows sharing a stamp are assumed to be uniformly spaced within the stamp: t = stamp + 0.01 x j/n (j = zero-based row index within the stamp, n = rows sharing it).
2. The conductivity record is converted with the configured equation and is flagged as unvalidated as solution conductivity until M2 and the probe checks decide otherwise.
3. NH4 values are in mg NH4+/L as confirmed by the experimenter (the file header says only `mg/L`).
