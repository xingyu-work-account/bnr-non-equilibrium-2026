## Model

State: true NH4 C(t) in mg N/L from day 1.0, +65.4643 mg N/L exactly at each pulse, dC/dt = −r ≥ 0. Rate laws: Haldane in free ammonia, r = vmax S/(Ks + S + S²/Ki) with S = FA(C, pH(t), temperature SCENARIO), fitted as α = vmax/Ks, Ks and S_opt = √(Ks Ki) (r = α S/(1 + S/Ks + S²/S_opt²), the same law; pass 4, Y6); first-order decay of biomass activity, r = v0 exp(−kd (t − 1)) C/(K_NH + C) with K_NH = 1.0 mg N/L fixed (ASM1 default, not fitted); combined, r = v0 exp(−kd (t − 1)) x Haldane(S); W5 substrate-side candidates: alkalinity-limited, r = v0 A/(K_ALK + A) C/(K_NH + C), and alkalinity x Haldane(S), with the alkalinity state A = Alk0 − 2 n/M_N (n = N consumed since day 1, 2 eq/mol N, nothing added), Alk0 fitted and K_ALK = 0.5 meq/L fixed (ASM3 default); and a no-consumption reference. Observation: log R = a + (b0 + b1 (t − 1)) log C + offsets at every electrode-offset jump detected in M2 (8; the M2 detections are used), then a first-order lag τ (exact exponential filter). Residuals in log R on the 0.01 d grid.

Fixed constants and scope (pass 6, G6 and G7). K_NH and K_ALK are ASSUMPTIONS, not estimates, and the inverted C(t) inherits K_NH through the joint fit of the selected model. The W5 state A = Alk0 − 2 n/M_N is a closed alkalinity balance without CO2 exchange or an inorganic-carbon term, so these candidates test an alkalinity limitation, not an inorganic-carbon limitation. Every M6 table names its NH4 observation model and model-state series (`nh4_observation_model`, `nh4_series`; per column in `model_response_fractions.csv`) and, in `assumptions`, every fixed constant and SCENARIO it rests on.

What the (α, Ks, S_opt) form did and did not do (pass 5, Z3; finite-difference Jacobian at the converged alkalinity_FA_SCENARIO_T25C calibration, start x0.5: condition number 4.2e6, |corr(Ks, S_opt)| 0.89): it removed the near-perfect correlations of (vmax, Ks, Ki) (largest kinetic |corr| 0.99998 → 0.89 at the alkalinity x FA optimum), but not the ill-conditioning. The natural-unit Haldane parameters of the alkalinity x FA candidates (vmax about 2,600 mg N/L/d, Ki about 0.005 mg NH3/L at that calibration) are therefore not estimates; only their skills and Alk0 are reported.

Interpretation, conditional on the model: the absolute NH4 level (C0) and the electrode intercept a are not identifiable, so the model does not determine absolute NH4 (NO-GO for absolute concentrations stands). The dose anchors the response, and the declining response fractions are explained by accumulation of NH4 under a log-type electrode with a non-drifting gain b, together with decaying activity, not by a drifting gain. The lag τ cannot reproduce the 7.5 h delayed jumps (a pure delay), and the residuals stay strongly autocorrelated: the model is misspecified at short time scales.
