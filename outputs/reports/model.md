## Model

State: true NH4 C(t) in mg N/L from day 1.0, +65.4643 mg N/L exactly at each pulse, dC/dt = −r ≥ 0. Rate laws: Haldane in free ammonia, r = vmax S/(Ks + S + S²/Ki) with S = FA(C, pH(t), temperature SCENARIO); first-order decay of biomass activity, r = v0 exp(−kd (t − 1)) C/(K_NH + C) with K_NH = 1.0 mg N/L fixed (ASM1 default, not fitted); combined, r = v0 exp(−kd (t − 1)) x Haldane(S); W5 substrate-side candidates: alkalinity-limited, r = v0 A/(K_ALK + A) C/(K_NH + C), and alkalinity x Haldane(S), with the alkalinity state A = Alk0 − 2 n/M_N (n = N consumed since day 1, 2 eq/mol N, nothing added), Alk0 fitted and K_ALK = 0.5 meq/L fixed (ASM3 default); and a no-consumption reference. Observation: log R = a + (b0 + b1 (t − 1)) log C + offsets at every electrode-offset jump detected in M2 (8; the M2 detections are used), then a first-order lag τ (exact exponential filter). Residuals in log R on the 0.01 d grid.

Interpretation, conditional on the model: the absolute NH4 level (C0) and the electrode intercept a are not identifiable, so the model does not determine absolute NH4 (NO-GO for absolute concentrations stands). The dose anchors the response, and the declining response fractions are explained by accumulation of NH4 under a log-type electrode with a non-drifting gain b, together with decaying activity, not by a drifting gain. The lag τ cannot reproduce the 7.5 h delayed jumps (a pure delay), and the residuals stay strongly autocorrelated: the model is misspecified at short time scales.

## Single NH4 observation model: inverted C(t) (W1)

Every downstream NH4 quantity is derived from C(t) obtained by inverting this observation model with the whole-record refit (quasi-static inversion C = exp((log R − a − offsets)/b(t)) of the 0.01 d grid reading; the sensor lag τ is not inverted (slopes after ~3τ are unaffected, window starts within 3τ of a boundary carry the lag transient)). Its uncertainty band is the envelope of the inversions under every converged profile vector with Δ ≤ 3.84 (`nh4_truth_vectors.csv`). Because C0 and the intercept a are not identifiable, the band is open on the side(s) listed below: the data do not bound the absolute level there, and the band edge is only the last evaluated vector. `outputs/processed/nh4_truth.parquet` holds the series.
