## Oxygen and energy metric family (W4)

Per pulse: N removed, O2 required (4.57 g O2/g N; 3.43 as a nitritation case), O2 transfer capacity kLa (C* − DO) V Δt per kLa_O2 SCENARIO (DO = 0 while not measured), utilization = required/capacity (a scenario whose capacity is below the requirement is `not computed: SCENARIO infeasible`), aeration-equivalent energy = O2/AE per field-AE SCENARIO (demand-based floor and supply-based value, in kWh/kg N), the free energy released over that energy, the W3 idle fraction as bounds (LOWER_BOUND: inactive bins over all bins; UPPER_BOUND: inactive, unclassified and artifact bins over all bins) with its coverage (`classified_fraction`) and, only where the coverage is at least one half, the CONDITIONAL value over classified time, and the dose-removal time (dose / post-pulse rate, not a fitted relaxation constant) against the pulse interval. The supply-based energy divides a transfer capacity at DO = 0 by a field AE defined at DO = 2 mg/L. The stirrer terms are LABORATORY-SPECIFIC.
