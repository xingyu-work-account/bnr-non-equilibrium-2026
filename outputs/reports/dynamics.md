## Application to the data

Windows never span a pulse.

Stationarity: ADF p < 0.05 and KPSS p > 0.05 on the raw window; nothing is detrended (detrending a quantized series creates a sawtooth and spurious irreversibility; an earlier version of this stage did so and was corrected before reporting).
