## Segmentation rule

Segments start at day 1.0 (start-up excluded) and at each pulse (8.53, 10.57, 24.59, 32.39), and end at 47.92. Every NH4 step-detector candidate of M2 with |step| ≥ 4 mg NH4+/L splits a segment: located, sustained jumps (electrode offsets and delayed pulse responses) at their 10-90 % midpoint, and unlocated or non-sustained candidates at their rolling-step peak, so no slope window spans a pulse or any detected step. The first 0.1 d after each boundary is excluded; a sub-segment needs at least 0.25 d of fit window. Slopes are Theil-Sen (`scipy.stats.theilslopes`) on the 0.01 d grid bins lying wholly inside the fit window.

**Caveat.** The Theil-Sen 95 % intervals assume independent residuals; the NH4 residuals are strongly autocorrelated (lag-1 0.82 to 0.998 in the kinetic fits), so these intervals are too narrow.

## Kinetic models

Monod uses the exact Lambert-W solution; Haldane uses free ammonia (observed pH, temperature SCENARIO) as substrate and inhibitor, integrated with RK4 on the grid. A parameter is not identifiable if at a bound, if its relative SE (from the Jacobian) exceeds 1.0, or if JᵀJ is ill-conditioned.

**AIC comparisons are not reliable here**: every model leaves strongly autocorrelated residuals (Ljung-Box p ≈ 0), which violates the independence that AIC assumes. Lowest-AIC models are listed for completeness only.

## Change points (PELT)

`ruptures.Pelt(model='linear', min_size=25, jump=5)` on each grid series with [t, 1] as covariates (discontinuous piecewise-linear, so drift does not create breaks). Penalty 3 σ_p² ln n (BIC, three parameters per break). The residual r about a 101-bin rolling median is strongly autocorrelated, so σ_p is autocorrelation-aware: r is prewhitened with its lag-1 autocorrelation φ (e = r_t − φ r_{t−1}), σ_e is the robust scale of e, and σ_p = σ_e/(1 − φ) is the AR(1) long-run scale. A break is an event if its level discontinuity is at least 5 σ (σ = marginal robust scale of r). Tied to a pulse: -0.02 to +0.5 d after it.
