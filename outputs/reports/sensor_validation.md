## NH4+ jump detection

Detector: step(t) = median over (t, t + 0.05 d] − median over (t − 0.05 d, t] on the reconstructed native series; candidates where abs(step) ≥ 4.0 mg NH4+/L, merged within 0.1 d. Each candidate is located at the 50 % crossing (`t_jump_d`) of a 2min rolling median between pre and post levels taken 0.05 to 0.15000000000000002 d away. A candidate is a jump if sustained (abs(post − pre) ≥ 4.0) and located. A rising jump -0.02 to 0.5 d after a pulse is a pulse response; every other jump is an electrode offset. Transition times shorter than the 14.4-min stamp rely on the uniform-within-stamp assumption; a transition of one sample interval (about 0.33 min at 20 s) means the jump falls between two consecutive rows.
