## 4. Hand recomputation

Done independently with literature constants at 25 °C (pK1 = 6.352, log KH = −1.468, M_N = 14.007, pCO2 = 4.2×10⁻⁴ atm from `config`):

- **Bound.** N_nit = M_N × Alk0 / 2, with Alk0 in meq/L, mmol N = meq/2, and N in mg N/L. This gives 14.01, 35.02 and 70.03 against the code's 14.007, 35.018 and 70.035 ✓.
- **HCO3−.** CO2(aq) = KH·pCO2 = 0.01430 mmol/L. HCO3− = CO2(aq)·10^(pH−pK1) = 0.4011 at pH 7.8 and 0.6357 at pH 8.0 (mmol/L). The code gives 0.4013 and 0.6360 ✓ (within 0.001; the remaining difference comes from constant rounding).
- **FA.** NH3 fraction = 10^7.8 / (e^(6344/298.15) + 10^7.8) = 0.03497, so FA = 17/14 × 200 × 0.03497 = 8.49 mg NH3/L. The code gives 8.492 ✓.
- **Aeration-equivalent energy.** **Not verifiable.** No SAE scenario, SAE value or aeration-equivalent energy exists anywhere in `src/` or `outputs/`. This acceptance criterion is unverified.

Either the inversion level (a and C0 are not identifiable; the band is open upward) is wrong, or Alk0 exceeds 20 meq/L, or a non-proton N sink exists. Pulse 3's constant-volume η of −0.18 is correctly withheld, but it shows that the inversion breaks the N mass balance.

- Consistency checks: σ ≥ 0 sits under "Consistency checks" in the model section ✓, but the coexistence affinity and ΔA are still presented as GO findings (R7).
